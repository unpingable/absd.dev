Manual

These are the 17 manual pages installed on the operator image under /share/man. Start with intro(1) and afterboot(8).

MILESTONE-QUALIFIED Rendered when this site is built from the unmodified mdoc(7) sources at milestone/general-usability-qemu-20260927 (platform ff025b1), with GNU groff version 1.23.0 and the arguments the image build uses (-mdoc -Tascii -P-cbou -rHY=0 -dAD=l). The text is what man prints in an ABSD session; only cross-references are turned into links. The pages describe the qualified QEMU system and label what is not provided. There are no per-command pages beyond date and uptime yet.

Read the pages as dated 2026-09-27. They are the milestone's pages, not the source release's: the release carries a later manual under platform/man/, with pages that are not here. Their build commands and their references to files "in the kernel repository" or "in the platform repository" describe the development repositories at the milestone; in the public tree those are the kernel/ and platform/ directories at later revisions, and the way to build is the repository's README. Later work, including the first boots on a physical machine and the release's lower connection limit, is on the status page, not here. One known error: e1000(4) says the network address is compiled in; network.conf(5) is correct.

ABSD is an experimental operating system by James Beck, built largely by AI coding agents under the author's direction and not independently audited. Its source is public under Apache-2.0 (source release absd-preview-20261010, what that does and does not establish); it runs under QEMU only, and no physical hardware is supported. This site's source is separate.
