The index of manual pages is the manual.

ABSD is an experimental operating system by James Beck, built largely by AI coding agents under the author's direction and not independently audited. Its source is public under Apache-2.0 (source release absd-preview-20261010, what that does and does not establish); it runs under QEMU only, and no physical hardware is supported. This site's source is separate.

Facts on this site are stated as of milestone/general-usability-qemu-20260927 (kernel 1983172, platform ff025b1) unless labelled otherwise. Those revisions are earlier than the source release. How statements are labelled.
