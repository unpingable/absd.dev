Read the pages as dated {{MILESTONE_DATE}}. They are the milestone's pages, not the source release's: the release carries a later manual under platform/man/, with pages that are not here. Their build commands and their references to files "in the kernel repository" or "in the platform repository" describe the development repositories at the milestone; in the public tree those are the kernel/ and platform/ directories at later revisions, and the way to build is the repository's README. Later work, including the first boots on a physical machine and the release's lower connection limit, is on the status page, not here.
